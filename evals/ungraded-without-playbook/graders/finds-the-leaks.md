The response identifies, in substance, at least TWO of the ways this
drafting lets the payout be reduced:
  - the accounting policies are the Buyer's "as applied from time to
    time", so they are not locked to pre-closing practice and the number
    can be moved;
  - set-off is available for claims that have NOT been finally
    determined, which turns the earn-out into something the Buyer can
    withhold at will;
  - the Buyer determines the amount, with no stated covenant to run the
    business consistently or any express protection against conduct that
    reduces the earn-out.
