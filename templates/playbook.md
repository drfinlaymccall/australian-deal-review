# Earn-out playbook

**Counsel fills this in.** Until they do, draft reviews can describe
clauses but cannot grade them, and will say so.

The point of the playbook is to use counsel's judgement once and then
apply it on every turn without paying for it again. Counsel sets the
standard here; the review applies it and sends back only what breaches
it.

For each item: **Red line** (we do not sign), **Fallback** (acceptable
with the stated change), **Must-have** (we need this added).
